## Project Overview (complete)

*1.2 Brief History 
Resting Hearts Funeral Parlor is a small business founded in 2026 by a local community 
Software Developer in Johannesburg, Gauteng. The business provides respectful and 
affordable funeral arrangements to families and individuals who have lost a loved one. Its 
services include funeral arrangements, coffins and caskets, transportation,funeral 
programs and related support.
